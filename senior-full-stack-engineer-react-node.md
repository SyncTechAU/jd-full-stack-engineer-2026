# Senior Full-Stack Engineer - React / Node

**SyncTech** | Remote (Australia only) | Full-time

---

## About Sync Technologies

Insurance decisions today are often made on fragmented information with little visual context. SyncTech changes that.
Our platform combines visual-first workflows, digital twins, AI-driven damage detection, and remote inspection technology to help insurers, builders, and assessors make faster, clearer, and more defensible decisions.

We are past early product–market fit and already working with major enterprise insurers in Australia and Europe. Now we are entering the next phase of our journey: scaling adoption, expanding product capabilities, and building the category leader in visual property intelligence.
We are building a company for the long term - and looking for people who want to help shape it.

---

## The Role

We're looking for a Full-Stack Engineer to join a small, focused engineering team. You'll work across our React frontend and Node services - a GraphQL BFF and NestJS REST APIs - to build and improve the core platform, with a particular focus on our 2026 AI damage detection initiatives.

This is a hands-on role. You'll write production code every day, contribute to architecture decisions, and work directly with the product and operations teams to solve real problems for our customers.

---

## What You'll Do

- Work with product, sales and operations to understand the problem and the outcome we need, then ship the smallest change that moves us towards that outcome, and iterate.
- Help deliver our 2026 and 2027 AI initiatives, including computer vision pipelines for property damage detection. You are not expected to be an ML engineer; you will work closely with one.
- Understand the platform's limits and drive the work that keeps it fast and reliable as we scale data volumes and customer load.
- Uphold and improve the high bar for quality across the codebase through knowledge sharing, tests and code review.
- Ship with Claude Code daily as writing assistance, but own the authorship: the code you merge is yours to understand, defend and maintain.

Our stack: React, TypeScript, Vite and Tailwind for the customer-facing web apps and internal tooling, Node/NestJS and TypeScript for the backend services and their GraphQL and REST APIs, PostgreSQL for data, AWS provisioned with Terraform, and GitHub Actions for CI/CD.

---

## What We're Looking For

**Must-have**

- Production code shipped with LLM coding tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Copilot or equivalent) while keeping code ownership: you can explain, defend and maintain every line you merged.
- 5+ years building production systems in React, Node and TypeScript.
- Designing and evolving GraphQL and REST APIs.
- PostgreSQL, or another relational database used in production.
- AWS or another major cloud, provisioned with infrastructure as code (we use Terraform).
- CI/CD pipelines you have built or maintained, not just used.
- Clear written and verbal communication, in code review as much as in meetings.

**Senior signals we'll look for**

- Authored or led an architecture decision in the last 12 months.
- Carried a production on-call rotation.
- Mentored at least one mid-level engineer.
- Led an incident response or wrote its post-mortem.
- Shipped a non-trivial migration end-to-end, from proposal to decommissioning the old path.

**Bonus**

None of these affect whether you progress; they only separate otherwise equal candidates. If you tick every box above and none below, we still want to hear from you.

- Worked on AI/ML or computer vision pipelines, the centre of our 2026 and 2027 roadmap.
- Insurtech, proptech or enterprise SaaS domain experience.
- 3D imaging or photogrammetry.
