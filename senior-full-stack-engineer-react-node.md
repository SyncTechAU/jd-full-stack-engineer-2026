# Senior Full-Stack Engineer - React / Node

**SyncTech** | Remote (Australia only) | Full-time

---

## About Sync Technologies

Insurance decisions today are often made on fragmented information with little visual context. SyncTech changes that.
Our platform combines visual-first workflows, digital twins, AI-driven damage detection, and remote inspection technology to help insurers, builders, and assessors make faster, clearer, and more defensible decisions.

We are past early product–market fit and already working with major enterprise insurers in Australia and Europe. Now we are entering the next phase of our journey: scaling adoption, expanding product capabilities, and building the category leader in visual property intelligence.
We are building a company for the long term - and looking for people who want to help shape it.

---

## The Role

We're looking for a Full-Stack Engineer to join a small, focused engineering team. You'll work across our React frontend and Node services - a GraphQL BFF and NestJS REST APIs - to build and improve the core platform, with a particular focus on our 2026 AI damage detection initiatives.

This is a hands-on role. You'll write production code every day, contribute to architecture decisions, and work directly with the product and operations teams to solve real problems for our customers.

---

## What You'll Do

- We use Claude Code daily; we expect you to ship production code with LLM assistance and to know when not to.
- Build and maintain our customer-facing services and internal tooling using React and Node with TypeScript.
- Design and implement scalable backend services and GraphQL and RESTful APIs.
- Contribute to architecture decisions and establish engineering practices that the team can build on.
- Work closely with product and operations to turn business requirements into well-designed technical solutions.
- Help deliver our 2026 AI initiatives, including computer vision pipelines for property damage detection.
- Optimise platform performance and reliability as we scale data volumes and customer load.
- Uphold and improve the high bar for quality across the codebase through tests, code review, and knowledge sharing.
